Getting here

A short walk from the station.

01

By train

Five to seven minutes on foot from Leeds City station. Out the south side, over the river, follow Sovereign Street round to The Calls.

02

By bus

Most city-centre routes stop at Boar Lane or the bus station — both about ten minutes away on foot.

03

By car

No parking at the bar — the nearest car parks are Q-Park The Light and Trinity Leeds, both a five-minute walk.
