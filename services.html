Beyond the bar

Other things we’re good for.

01

Sport on the screens

Sky Sports and ESPN across multiple screens, sound up for the big games. Group of mates for the match? Give us a ring and we’ll save you a corner.

02

Pool tables

A proper table in the back room. First-come; busy on Friday and Saturday evenings.

03

The terrace

South-facing wooden benches right on the River Aire. Sunny afternoons, summer evenings, the best non-secret in the city. We don’t book it — just turn up and try your luck.

04

Groups & gatherings

Birthdays, after-work, leaving dos. The lounge area takes a crowd — ring ahead and tell us how many; we’ll point you to the right spot.
