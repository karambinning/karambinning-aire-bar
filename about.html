About — The Calls, Leeds

A warehouse on the water.

Aire Bar sits below an old riverside warehouse on The Calls, in the kind of room you have to find the steps for.

The building

Grade II listed.
Brick-vaulted.
Properly old.

The Calls runs along the north bank of the River Aire, threaded with the brick warehouses Leeds was once full of. Aire Bar is in the basement of one of them — the building is Grade II listed, the cellars are still vaulted, the brickwork is still the brickwork. We’ve added warm light, leather chesterfields, a proper bar and a pool table; the building does the rest.

It’s a quieter corner of the city centre. Five minutes from the station, around the back of the Corn Exchange, but it feels removed once you’re down the steps and through the door.

The terrace

South-facing, on the river.

Walk through the bar and the room opens onto the terrace — right on the water, south-facing, wooden benches, the kind of light that makes a pint last twice as long. In summer it’s the best seat in Leeds; in winter the inside takes over and the room gets cosy in a way only an old cellar can.

The view runs straight up the river toward Centenary Bridge and the redeveloped Aire skyline. We like to think of it as the South of France on a good afternoon — without the flight.

What we’re for

A drinking den, first and last.

We’re not a cocktail bar, not a restaurant pretending to be a pub. We’re a proper drinking establishment with proper food — cask ales done properly, burgers done properly, a sofa to sink into and a pool table when you want one. The sport’s on; book ahead if it’s a big one.

Most of all we’re a place people come back to. Groups for the match. Mates after work. Couples who’ve spotted the terrace from across the water and come around to find the door. The room finds room for them all.
