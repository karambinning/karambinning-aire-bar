The Place

A vaulted basement with a view of the river.

Down a short flight of stone steps off The Calls, Aire Bar opens into the cellar of an old warehouse — bare brick arches, low warm light, chesterfield sofas you sink into. Walk through and the room opens onto a south-facing terrace right on the water; in summer the wooden benches fill up and the light off the river does the rest.

It’s a Grade II listed building, properly old, properly looked after. A drinking den first — with proper pub food, proper cask ales, and a pool table when you want one.

What we do

Four things, done well.

01

Real ales & continental beer

Saltaire Pride, Black Sheep and Ossett Yorkshire Blonde on the hand-pumps. Blue Moon, Fruli and Veltins for something colder. A full wine list and a proper spirits shelf behind the bar.

02

Pub food, burgers a specialty

Bacon, pork-and-chorizo and halloumi burgers, served with fries. Fish and chips. Vegetarian options. Lunch deal: two burgers and fries for the price of one.

03

Sports on the screens

Sky Sports and ESPN across multiple screens. A good place to bring a group for the match — book ahead for the big ones.

04

The terrace

South-facing, on the water, with wooden benches and proper afternoon sun. A bit of a Leeds secret — even on a hot day you can usually find a spot.
